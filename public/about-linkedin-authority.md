# About LinkedIn Authority Engine

Welcome to the **LinkedIn Authority Engine** — your ultimate AI-powered companion for building a professional brand as a software engineer.

## What is it?
The platform securely connects to your GitHub account, analyzes your code repositories, and generates highly engaging, technically accurate LinkedIn posts. Whether you are launching an open-source project, sharing a technical deep-dive, or highlighting a new feature, our AI engine understands the context of your code and translates it into professional narratives.

## Why use it?
- **Save Time:** Stop struggling with what to write. Let the AI draft your post in seconds.
- **Multi-lingual:** Generate posts natively in English, Arabic, or German.
- **Secure & Private:** We only process your data in real-time. No code is stored on our servers, and you can delete your account at any time.
